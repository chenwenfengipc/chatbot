## InvoiceKit (page: /products/invoicekit)
Online invoicing for small businesses: create invoices, send them by email, track payments.
Pricing (USD):
- Free: $0. Up to 5 invoices per month. Includes: email sending; PDF export.
- Pro: $12 per month, or $10 per month billed yearly. Unlimited invoices. Includes: recurring invoices; payment reminders; custom branding.
- Business: $29 per month, or $24 per month billed yearly. Includes everything in Pro; multiple users; API access.
- Enterprise: custom pricing - customers must contact sales.

## StockPilot (page: /products/stockpilot)
Inventory tracking for shops and warehouses: stock levels, low-stock alerts, barcode scanning.
Pricing (USD): $19 per month per location. No free plan.
