# Products
## ScriptStep (page: /products/scriptstep)
Record business processes in the browser and replay them as automated tests - no coding needed. The Chrome extension captures each step; the web app is where you refine, run and scale your tests.
Delivered as: Web App + Chrome Extension.
Features: No-code test automation - Record business processes in the browser and play them back as automated tests - no scripting required. | Visual step editor - Insert, edit, delete and reorder steps, and change input values, to keep tests in step with your system. | Automated test runner - Execute a single script on demand or run multiple scripts in batch, with execution logs for every run. | Reusable test templates - Standardise common processes once and reuse them across projects, clients and environments. | Pre-built ERP adapters - Adapters for Oracle Fusion Cloud, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and SAP. | Mass data import - Drive recorded processes with bulk data to load or update records at scale.
Typical uses: Regression testing during ERP implementations, upgrades and quarterly releases; Consultants reusing standard test templates across clients and environments; Bulk data loading through ERP screens during go-live and data migration.
Pricing (USD):
- Standard: $30/seat/month, or $24/seat/month billed yearly. Record and play back automated tests on any web application. Includes: No-code test recording & playback; Configurable playback settings; Script export, import & reset; Execution logs.
- Professional: $50/seat/month, or $40/seat/month billed yearly, minimum 5 seats. For teams that maintain tests and run them automatically. Includes: Everything in Standard; Visual step editor; Test data editing; Resume playback from any step; Automated test runner.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, minimum 50 seats - customers must contact sales. Includes: Everything in Professional; Pre-built ERP adapters; Reusable test templates; Batch test execution; Mass data import; Custom volume pricing.
Feature availability by plan: Sign-in with Google or Microsoft account [standard, professional, enterprise]; No-code test recording & playback [standard, professional, enterprise]; Configurable playback settings [standard, professional, enterprise]; Script export, import & reset [standard, professional, enterprise]; Execution logs [standard, professional, enterprise]; Resume playback from any step [professional, enterprise]; Visual step editor (insert, edit, delete & reorder steps) [professional, enterprise]; Test data editing (step input values) [professional, enterprise]; Reusable test templates [enterprise]; Automated test runner (single script) [professional, enterprise]; Batch test execution (multiple scripts) [enterprise]; Mass data import [enterprise]; Pre-built ERP adapters (Oracle Fusion Cloud, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, SAP) [enterprise].
Notes: Prices in USD per seat. Yearly plans are billed annually at 20% off the monthly price.

## UI Recorder (page: /products/ui-recorder)
A free Chrome extension that captures clicks, inputs and navigation as a clean, step-by-step recording - ready to turn into test scripts, training guides or bug reports.
Features: One-click recording - Start and stop capture from the toolbar - every click, keystroke and page change is logged in order. | Readable steps - Recordings are shown as human-readable steps, not raw events. | Export - Export recordings for test automation, SOP documentation or issue tickets. | Local-first - Recordings stay in your browser unless you choose to export them.
Typical uses: QA teams creating regression test scripts; Consultants documenting ERP configuration steps; Support teams reproducing user-reported bugs.
Price: free.

## Web WhatsApp Extension (page: /products/webwhatsapp-ext)
A Chrome extension that turns WhatsApp Web into a lightweight business tool: send broadcasts, let AI handle replies, translate conversations and export your contacts.
Features: Unlimited broadcasting - Send the same message to many contacts at once, with no cap on broadcasts. | AI auto-reply - Answer incoming messages automatically with AI - use your own AI provider or ours. | Built-in translation - Translate messages with Google Translate to chat with customers in any language. | Contact export - Export your WhatsApp contacts for your CRM or spreadsheets. | Works in your browser - Runs inside WhatsApp Web - no extra app to install.
Pricing (USD):
- BYAT (Bring Your AI & Translator): $5/month. Connect your own AI and Google Translate accounts and pay only for the extension. Includes: Single WhatsApp number; Unlimited broadcasting; Limited auto-reply (uses your AI); Export contacts; Limited translation (uses your Google Translate).
- WOAT (With Our AI & Translator): $10/month. Everything included. No API keys or setup needed - we provide the AI and translation. Includes: Single WhatsApp number; Unlimited broadcasting; 10,000 auto-replies (uses our AI); Export contacts; Unlimited translation (uses our Google Translate).
Notes: Prices in USD, billed monthly. Cancel anytime. BYAT requires your own AI provider and Google Translate API keys; their usage is billed to you by those providers.
Disclaimer: Web WhatsApp Extension is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc.
